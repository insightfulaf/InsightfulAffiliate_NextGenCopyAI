## Strategic Direction

Use the desktop HTML export as the current product direction source. The older `2026-Affiliate Product Creation Strategy.pdf` is no longer the primary automation reference.

InsightfulAffiliate should lead with beginner-friendly affiliate execution and trust-building. NextGenCopyAI should support that with conversion copy, prompts, and workflow assets that turn inputs into usable launch materials.

## Validated Product Lanes

1. `ai-affiliate-launch-os` - flagship beginner launch system for niche choice, offer scoring, landing pages, email capture, tracking, and compliance.
2. `aeo-geo-affiliate-content-kit` - AI-search-ready content templates for comparison pages, FAQ structures, source mapping, and proof gathering.
3. `nextgencopyai-conversion-prompt-vault` - focused prompts for reviews, landing pages, emails, comparison tables, objections, and brand voice rewrites.
4. `faceless-affiliate-content-studio` - content workflows for beginners who do not want video, voice, or personal-brand exposure.
5. `affiliate-offer-finder-profit-tracker` - scorecards and tracking assets for affiliate program selection and funnel fit.
6. `trust-first-affiliate-compliance-kit` - FTC disclosure templates, trust blocks, AI-use disclosure, safe claims, and review ethics assets.
7. `ai-copy-to-commission-email-system` - welcome, education, soft-sell, review-request, and follow-up email sequences.

## Automation Implications

- Funnel-map automation should organize generated snippets by these product lanes.
- Filename guessing is acceptable only as a fallback; human-editable hints in `docs/funnel_hints.json` are the preferred source of truth.
- Automation should generate launch assets for human review, not publish directly without approval.
- Priority should go to paste-ready snippets, lead magnets, email sequences, compliance blocks, and offer-tracking assets.
